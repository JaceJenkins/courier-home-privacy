Courier Home Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Courier Home is a private app made for one household. It backs up the household's iPhones to the household's own Mac at home and keeps their contacts in sync with it. It has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells or rents information to anyone.

What Courier Home sends

Each kind of information is off until you turn it on in the app.

  • Your contacts, including each contact's notes, so the backup is complete and a change made on either side is made on both.
  • The Apple Health data you choose, by group: training and body, recovery and vitals, intake and medications, clinical records, sexual activity and symptoms, and workout routes.
  • Files, links, notes and contact cards you choose to send with the share sheet.
  • For keeping the app working: the name of the device's key, the address Apple's push service uses to wake the app, each sync's times and results, whether the phone was too warm or in Low Power Mode, the app and iOS versions, and the app's own log. It collects no crash reports.

Where it is kept

On your iPhone, and on the household's own Mac at home. Courier Home sends straight to that Mac over an encrypted connection, at home or away, and to nowhere else. It has no servers of its own and no account. Before Courier Home changes a contact, it keeps a copy of the card on your iPhone.

Who can see it

Your household, on its own Mac. Courier Home sends nothing to the developer's or anyone else's servers, and shares your information with no one.

Contacts and their notes

With your permission, Courier Home reads and writes the Notes field of your contacts. It reads each contact, its note included, to back it up. It writes to the Notes field when a note was changed on the Mac and you allow Courier Home to update contacts: it saves the new note text into that contact on your iPhone. Notes are copied as they are, as plain text.

Apple Health

With your permission, Courier Home reads the Health data you choose and saves nothing to Health. Health information is never used for advertising or marketing, and is shared only as described above.

Your choices

  • Turn off any kind of information in Courier Home: each one has its own switch.
  • Change what Courier Home may read in the Health app: tap your profile picture, then Apps.
  • Change contacts access in Settings: Apps, then Courier.
  • Deleting the app removes the information kept on your phone.
  • To have your information removed from the household's Mac, or for any question about this policy, write to the address below.
